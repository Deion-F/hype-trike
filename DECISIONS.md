## 0b. Implementation choices (Claude's defaults; override any time)

| # | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I.1 | Use **pygame-ce with an asyncio main loop** instead of the `pgzrun.go()` runner. The Wireframe code gets refactored into it. | pygbag needs an `async` main loop that yields to the browser every frame. Pygame Zero's blocking runner doesn't fit that, and pygbag ships pygame-ce, so desktop and web use the same library. This is still the Python + pygbag path (Option A). |
| I.2 | Fixed logic resolution: **360 px tall**, with width set from the screen's aspect ratio (640 on 16:9, about 780 on a 19.5:9 phone) | "Smooth fill" with no stretching and no black bars: wider phones show more track ahead. |
| I.3 | Track column = **80 px** (8 per 640 px view) | Keeps the homage's "8 blocks on screen" rhythm (100 px in 800 px). |
| I.4 | Simulation runs at a **fixed 60 Hz**, deterministic and frame-based | The ghost-rider validator and the CPU rivals run the exact same physics as the player. |
| I.5 | All art and audio are **generated in code** (pixel art drawn with pygame, chiptune synthesized) | Original assets, nothing ripped, no asset pipeline needed. |

- 2026-11-14: **Bike XP, cosmetic-only coins, four heat zones, lives, slipstream** (user).
  - **Progression.** Every run race earns the bike you rode XP: (20 + 15 × depth), ×1.5 for a boss, half if you missed the target (`meta/bike_tune.xp_for_race`). It is banked after each race. Each bike spends its own XP on 9 upgrades of 5 levels each. The 5 old tuning stats and 6 Starter Kit items were folded in (Engine, Gearing, Cooling, Radiator, Gyro Bars, Soft Shocks, Quick Steer, Knobby Tyres, Track Boots); the kit's Sponsor Deal was dropped. A fresh bike sits a little under its catalogue stats, and level 5 goes past them. Coins buy only cosmetics. Bikes unlock by clearing biomes (trike: Valley, scrambler: Desert, sprinter: Night). Old saves convert once (`progression_v` 2): old tuning and kit levels become the new levels on every owned bike, and Sponsor Deal levels are refunded in coins.
  - **Garage.** Two tabs. BIKES has the bike chips, the upgrade list and stat bars side by side; pointing at an upgrade shows how far its next level moves its bar. STYLE has bike paint, rider and boost on one screen, with a big preview of the combination and the flame in all four heat zones.
  - **Heat zones (one-button).** 16 bars: cold 0–4, warm 4–20 (half the gauge, heats at ×0.75), hot 20–26 (+3 top speed, accel 20% quicker, air ×1.1, heats ×1.0), overheating 26–32 (+7.5 → +10.5 top speed, accel 45% quicker, air ×1.25, heats ×1.35, landing window ×0.75, on fire, blows up at the top). Was boost 9–24 at 48, then +1.5 → +6. Each zone also has its own engine sound layer: cold misfires, a warm whoosh, a hot supercharger whine, and an overheating warbling whine with crackle (`audio.zone_loop_gen`). Classic mode maps coast / A / turbo / red onto the same sounds.
